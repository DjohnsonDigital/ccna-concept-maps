MAP 33 QoS — Quality of Service
Core IdeaQoS prioritizes critical traffic. Classify → Mark → Queue → Schedule.
1. QoS Need
  • Bandwidth constraint causes drops
  • Voice/video needs low latency
  • Data can tolerate delay
  • Without QoS: all traffic equal
2. Marking
  • DSCP (6-bit): in IP header
  • CoS (3-bit): in 802.1Q tag
  • EF (46): voice/video
  • AF/CS classes for data
3. Queuing
  • FIFO: default (no priority)
  • WFQ: weighted fair queuing
  • CBWFQ: class-based WFQ
  • LLQ: low latency (voice)
4. Shaping vs Policing
  • Policing: drops/marks excess
  • Shaping: buffers/delays excess
  • Traffic contract (CIR)
  • Policing at ingress; shaping egress
