MAP 03 Cables & Interfaces
Core IdeaPhysical medium determines speed, distance, and duplex. Match cable to device type.
1. Copper UTP
  • Cat5e: 1G/100m
  • Cat6: 1G/100m (10G short)
  • Cat6a: 10G/100m
  • RJ-45 connector
2. UTP Wiring
  • Straight-through: diff devices
  • Crossover: same devices
  • T568A vs T568B
  • Auto-MDIX: auto-detect
3. Fiber Optic
  • SMF: long dist, laser, yellow
  • MMF: short dist, LED, orange
  • LC connector: small
  • SC/ST connectors: larger
4. Speeds
  • Ethernet: 10 Mbps
  • Fast Eth: 100 Mbps
  • Gigabit: 1 Gbps
  • 10G/25G/40G/100G: DC
